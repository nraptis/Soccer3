Eight rotation packs — 2026-11-03

Soccer now owns RotationPack mRotationPacks[8]. Each has a 16384-element
uint16_t permutation, a wrapping cursor, 4x4 selected frog windows, and two
masked byte rotations. No InitializeRotations or block-stage rotation
shuffle is used. Flat selected arrays remain as adapters to the plan API.

Each pack uses a disjoint 64 KiB slice from the last 128 KiB of the existing
256 KiB intermediate fold lanes A-D. Two packs use each lane. These bytes
are reused after Stage1 folding; no additional expander work is performed.
A 16-bit rejection-sampled Fisher-Yates pass must complete, then the
remaining slice supplies the byte offsets. Exhaustion fails rather than
using a partial shuffle or relaxing exclusions.

Global rules: exclude zero, half-turn, duplicates, window complements,
and actual-byte inverse windows. Each frog's four offsets plus its layer's
two masked offsets must have all 64 subset sums distinct in both rings.
Each layer has at least one odd byte rotation. Local sums are not globally
blocked. One candidate search wraps but stops after one complete traversal.

Twenty focused XCTest cases passed: RotationWindowTests (5),
EncryptionVaultPlanTests (5), EncryptionVaultTests (6),
FlameToadCipherTests (3), MaskCollisionTests (1). This includes actual
password/nonce seeding and reconstruction at all strengths, independent
blocks, literal-reference ciphertext checks, and tests that every chosen
rotation reaches the final cipher.

A standalone ASan+UBSan check farmed and independently validated 1000 full
eight-pack plans. No failures. Maximum candidate reads per pack: 22.
Mean reads per pack: 18.236. Maximum entropy used: 46250 of 65536 bytes.
Native build: Apple clang, C++20, -O2, address/undefined sanitizers.

Removed only the unused Soccer mask-bank buffer/selection/shuffle plumbing.
SoccerMaskBank and the active plan's derived complementary masks remain.
Final folded words now serve material-pointer shuffling and reversals.

The rotation policy and seed allocation change ciphertext. Historical
ciphertext requires its original implementation. This work validates the
new allocation and correctness, not cryptographic security. The full
statistical/parity campaigns have not been rerun for the new policy.
